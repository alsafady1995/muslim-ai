# 6. Competitor positioning: Tarteel, Quran Companion, Muslim Pro, Quran Majeed

Facts come from the competitive analysis on branch `claude/project-thread-ewg32p` (App Store listings read on
24 November 2026). Re-check prices and ratings before quoting any of them publicly; they change weekly.

**Rule of engagement:** we win by being *different*, not by attacking. Never name a competitor in store metadata
(App Store 2.3.7, Play policy) or in paid ads. On our own site and in PR, compare categories with dated facts. In a
community where all these apps serve the same Quran, disrespect costs more than it wins.

## 6.2 Each competitor

### Tarteel: the real rival

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they are** | AI memorization app with live mistake detection while you recite. 4.5★ (14K) in Saudi Arabia, #1 in Books there. SAR 44.99/month, SAR 349.99/year; a higher tier above that |
| **Strengths (be honest)** | Live correction, including some tashkeel errors. Years of production data. Mushaf page view. Strong brand among English-speaking Muslims |
| **Weaknesses** | Price: SAR 349.99/year is a tutor's price for many Gulf families with several children. Users report repeated misses on some ayahs. English-first brand ("Tarteel AI") in an Arabic market |
| **Blind spots** | **Forgetting.** They manage *today's* recitation, not *next month's* retention: no forgetting model, no at-risk ayahs, no review scheduling driven by your grades. Their memorization ends where real hifz begins: review |
| **Positioning gap** | "The coach for the 80% of hifz that is review." Nobody owns review |
| **How we win** | 1) Own **review and retention** (at-risk ayahs, spaced repetition, graded tests). 2) **Price**: about a third in Saudi Arabia. 3) **Tasmee' culture and Arabic-first brand**: "أسمِعني" is a word, not a product name. 4) **Privacy**: recordings deleted after analysis, said plainly |
| **Where we must not fight (yet)** | Live correction and accuracy claims. Until the benchmark is done and live test mode ships, any head-to-head on "catching mistakes" loses. Our ads never say "more accurate" |
| **Their likely response** | Price cuts in the Gulf, or adding spaced review. Our defence is the compounding data of each user's review history, and being the Arabic-native brand |

### Quran Companion: not a live competitor

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they are** | Hifz app with "swipe to reveal" self-testing, group challenges and a hasanah leaderboard. 4.5★ (112). **Last updated June 2018**; a 2025 review says it crashes when starting memorization |
| **Strengths** | Two good ideas: group challenges and simple self-testing |
| **Weaknesses** | Abandoned. No AI, no listening, crashes |
| **Blind spots** | Everything after 2018 |

| **How we win** | Do not mention it. Take its best idea: **halaqa and family challenges** (see `07-virality.md` §7.5), done on a maintained app with automatic grading. Bid on its users only through organic search ("hifz app", "حفظ القرآن"), never by name |

### Muslim Pro: the giant, on a different job

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they are** | The everything app: prayer times, athan, qibla, Quran, duas, community, an AI chatbot since Ramadan 2024. 4.7★ (602K). Ads with third-party tracking; Premium (≈ $34.99/year) removes them |
| **Strengths** | Opened five times a day for prayer times. Huge trust by habit. Breadth |
| **Weaknesses** | Ads and tracking next to the Quran, a recurring complaint in this community. Cluttered. Its Quran experience is a reader, not a coach. Its AI chatbot is a general assistant |
| **Blind spots** | It does not listen to you, and it has no memorization system. Its users who are serious about the Quran are underserved |
| **Positioning gap** | "Calm and focused" vs "everything plus ads" |
| **How we win** | **Complement, don't replace.** Our message to its users: "Keep your prayer-times app. Use Asmi3ni for your Quran." Lead with *calm*: no ads, no feed, one job done well. Our AI difference is grounding (cited sources, uncited claims removed), shown in screenshot 7, not argued |
| **Watch out** | We have no prayer times. Do not imply we replace it |

### Quran Majeed: the library

| | |
|---|---|

## 6.3 The positioning strategy

### The position statement (internal)

> For Arabic-speaking Muslims who memorized, or are memorizing, the Quran and have no one to recite to,
> **Asmi3ni is the recitation coach that listens to you and remembers what you are about to forget.**
> Unlike all-in-one Quran apps, it checks you instead of only playing to you; unlike other recitation coaches, it
> manages the review that keeps hifz alive, at a fraction of the price, with no ads.

### The one line (public)

> **المصحف تقرؤه في أي تطبيق. أسمِعني هو الذي تسمّع له.**
> *You can read the Quran in any app. Asmi3ni is the one you recite to.*

### The three pillars (every asset maps to one)

| Pillar | Proof in the app | What we say | What we never say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **يسمعك / It listens** | Word-by-word result, unclear vs wrong | سمّع حفظك متى شئت | "live", "real-time", "most accurate" |
| **يتذكّر عنك / It remembers for you** | Spaced repetition, at-risk ayahs, AI test → schedule | احفظ… ولا تنسَ | "guaranteed", "never forget" |
| **يحترمك / It respects you** | No ads, recordings deleted, honest paywall, grounded answers | بلا إعلانات، وتسجيلاتك تُحذف | "no tracking at all", "scholar-approved" (until true) |

### Category design

Do not try to be "a better Quran app". That category is owned by apps with 600K ratings. Create and name a smaller
category we can own on day one: **«تطبيق تسميع» / "a tasmee' app"**. Use the word "تسميع" in the name, the subtitle,
the first line of every description, and the landing page. When people search for it, we are the only answer that is
literally named for it.

### Sequencing (what makes the position stronger over time)

| When | Move | Why it widens the gap |
|---|---|---|
| Before paid launch | Accuracy benchmark + "It was right" tap | Lets us talk about accuracy with evidence, the one thing Tarteel can attack |
| Before Ramadan 2027 | Live test mode, Mushaf page view, free offline reading | Closes Tarteel's two real advantages; free offline undercuts Muslim Pro Premium |
| Ramadan 2027 | Ramadan plan + share cards | Seasonal demand + organic loop no one else runs well |
| After D30 retention is known | Family plan, halaqa dashboard | No competitor has it; it matches how hifz actually happens in the Gulf |
